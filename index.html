PROCESS

How we’ll work together

Four steps, each one agreed before moving on to the next.

  1. STEP 01

    Clarify the brief

    You tell me what you’re after; I confirm the scope, timeline and a rough quote.

  2. STEP 02

    Agree a plan

    I share a simple outline of the approach or a sketch of the page structure, and start once you’re happy with it.

  3. STEP 03

    Build

    You get progress updates along the way and can suggest changes at any point.

  4. STEP 04

    Deliver

    Working code or deployable files, with brief notes on how to use them.

NOTECommunication is mainly by email, so the brief, the quote and any revisions are all on record and easy to look back on.
